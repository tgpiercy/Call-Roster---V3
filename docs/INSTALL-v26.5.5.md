The integrity and 3 AM fixes before the alpha, from the Oct 19-20 reviews, plus **one rule change: the 16 h rule**. Brief: `briefs/v26.5.5.md` (the decisions inside; Graeme's three answers of Oct 21: only the work BEFORE an offer counts toward the 16 h, the master is on a share so the stale-copy guard uses the file's time, the Unavailable reason "Days" is "Working days elsewhere"). ## What's new
- **The 16 h rule (item 9; a rule change).** Overtime a person has already accepted, a posting awarded to them or a switch they work on the shift that TOUCHES the one on offer counts as worked time. The work that ends when the offer starts plus the offer may not pass 16 consecutive hours, and the offer must leave 11 hours before their next work (scheduled or already accepted). Only when nothing fits is the person off the list ("16 h rule" in the left-off summary); otherwise they stay listed with **[max N h]** on the row and the card: **A** (the whole offer) is refused with a box, **P** up to that many hours is accepted; on the award pop-up "max N h (16 h rule)" shows in the result column and a full award or a partial above it is refused. A switch is a whole shift, so it must fit. The rotation's own rules (scheduled, coming off nights, starting days) come first and are unchanged.
- **The night in progress (item 10).** Before 7 AM the call-out and sick-call date boxes default to **yesterday** - the night under way is dated by the evening it started - and the hint names it ("Night of Mon Oct 19 (7 PM Oct 19 - 7 AM Oct 20) - the shift under way now (3:10 AM)"). New Posting names the night in its hours hint. One date format everywhere (ddd mmm d).
- **A sick call asks before it saves (item 11):** "Book <name> sick for <date> <shift>, <unit>? They come off call lists until <day> 7 PM / 7 AM." No saves nothing. A supervisor's success line ends "Wrong person? Ask <admin> to void it." (the undo stays an admin's).
- **The Hours box fills itself (item 12):** 12, or the DST night's 13 / 11, on every date / shift change unless you typed; SAVE + START CALL-OUT carries the sick call's hours into the call-out.
- **Old Kronos hours are a red line for supervisors (item 13),** not a Yes / No they cannot act on: "Worked hours are N days old: the call order may be off. Tell <admin> to import Kronos." on the call-out and posting pop-ups. An admin keeps the Yes / No, Yes the default.
- **Every submitted session ends with what to do next (item 14):** the line is covered; or closed at N h (a partial: the rest dropped, nobody charged); or stays OPEN - call back later, post the shift, or ask the admin to award out of department.
- **The text pass (item 15):** a title on every box (147 of them); the admin is named by the setting, never "me"; no internal names in what a supervisor can meet (the supervisors' list, the one-time macros framed as "an admin runs ... once", the settings); a hint that asked for the VBA editor now names the button that changes the sheet and, for a repair by hand, the new admin macro **Alt+F8 > UnlockForRepair** (one sheet, then ProtectAll); the two Yes / No boxes with invented meanings are questions with Yes / No answers; "use NO TAKERS / CANCEL REQUEST" and "void that booking" tell a supervisor whom to ask; the Unavailable reason **"Days" is "Working days elsewhere"**.
- **The keys say what they cost (item 16):** "R Refused / charged x1.75", "L Left message / charged x1.75", "U Unavailable / not charged", "P Part of the hours / closes the shift", "K Call back"; the award preview reads "Signed up: not charged" / "Not signed up: 21 charged (12 h x 1.75)".
- **The trust strip (item 17):** "Worked hours through <date> (Kronos) | N totals with provisional OT | Refused counted to the current pay period (from <date>) | Members' file updated <when>" on the call-out and award pop-ups, the printed call-out roster's header, the board's Drawn line and the month PDF's footer. The card reads "141 h (to Oct 18)"; Find a person's status line too.
- **Practice on a copy (item 18):** a button under HELP on both Desks (supervisors too). The copy opens with a red **PRACTICE COPY: nothing here counts** banner on every sheet and COPY in the window title; copies older than a day are deleted the next time the roster is opened.
- **The integrity fixes (items 1-8):** a save that fails is **reported** - "NOT SAVED to disk (<why>). Don't close Excel. Try File > Save ..." - the call-out form keeps its entries on screen with SUBMIT locked (they are logged in the workbook; File > Save is the way out), the award pop-up's finished line says it, the Desk header reads "NOT SAVED since <time>" in red until a save goes through, and a close that could not save is cancelled with the same box; **a file someone else saved while you had it open refuses to be overwritten** ("Close WITHOUT saving, reopen, and log your entry again"); Esc during a write lands in the handler (never a half-written workbook with the sheets unprotected); a #REF! / #NAME? in Total Hours refuses to load the list (it sorted as 0 and put the person at the top) and refuses to publish; a rename rewrites Worked Periods too and the preview counts inbox sign-up files under the old name; a save block set by another failed check is no longer lifted by the layout macro; the OT Log's "rows written but not finished" case blocks the save (no duplicate session on a re-submit); a void that stops part-way blocks the save and names the key; the pay-period cap stays inside the roster's year and a PC clock in the past asks an admin; a Kronos file date outside the year is replaced by today, said so; "on the roster twice" is a PROBLEM in IntegrityCheck; SystemCheck shows the members' folder and the stale-copy guard.

## The panel
1. Open the roster (ADMIN view), then File > Open `Roster Updater.xlsm` in the same Excel window. Click the roster so it is in front.
2. Alt+F8 > `'Roster Updater.xlsm'!InstallPanel`. Pick the unzipped `Callout_Roster_v26.5.5` folder.
3. Click the one blue button, read its box, click the next. Group 3: System check ("25 of 25 on v26.5.5", ThisWorkbook v26.5.5, every form but frmEmployee v26.5.5), Self test (684 / 79). Group 4: the practice copy + SmokeTest (20 steps), the 3 AM date, the sick-call confirm, the 16 h rule's max N h, the Kronos-stale red line, the key labels and the trust strip, the Practice button, the failed-save box, the stale-copy refusal, the Unavailable reasons, then IntegrityCheck.

By hand instead: remove and import the 25 modules, re-paste ThisWorkbook and the eight forms (every one but frmEmployee) from `paste-in/`, Debug > Compile; then the group 3-4 rows yourself.
