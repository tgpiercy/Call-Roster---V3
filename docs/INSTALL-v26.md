# Call Out Roster v26 - install (the Install panel)

The BETA BUILD (`docs/timeline-2026-10-18.md`): the reports, minimum staffing, the members' follow-up M3 and the alpha's findings, presented to management and the union Nov 15. Brief: `briefs/v26.md` (the decisions inside). Revisions after the review are v26.5 and count up.

Needs **v25.5 installed** and **Roster Updater.xlsm v4.1**. **The updater does not change.** The panel's swap replaces every module (all 25 stamps on v26; no new module; no form and no ThisWorkbook change), and **rebuilds the members' template** from `member/` (Members M3): the panel's `template` row does it, then Member View publishes. **No rule changed**: SelfTest gains six suites (payback, idmark, prechange, chargewhy, minstaff, withdrawn) and more privacy cases: 644 cases, 74 suites. SmokeTest stays 19 steps.

**Back up first. Do it on a copy before the real roster.** The panel's group 3 runs `ApplyRosterLayout` once (the bar gains Employee history under Find a person) and asks you to check the **Min Staff** sheet (made with the plant's minimums on your first open after the install).

## What's new
- **Employee history (kit item 1).** Employee history on the bar's FIND group or the Desk: part of a name, a From and To date (the boxes open on the whole roster year), and the **History Report** tab lists every OT Log entry for that person, newest first, with their call-order place, the outcome, the hours charged and a **Why** line for each ("Charged 21 h: 3rd on the call list, Refused, 12 h offered x 1.75 (grade P2)"), then the charged hours per pay period and the total; Save a PDF as well? An admin is asked **Full** (who logged each entry and when, void rows struck through; it draws on the admin-only `History Report (admin)` tab) or **member-safe** (the columns the members' file shows, so it matches My history there); a supervisor gets the member-safe one.
- **"Why was I charged" (4.6).** Click any OT Log row: the status bar explains that row's charge from its own fields. The **Refusals Report** gains a second part under the totals, "The charges behind the figures": one line per charged row with the sentence. The members' file carries the same sentence for every charged row (below).
- **The two-week board (kit 3).** **Board** on the Calendar's top row (supervisors and admins): the Board tab for today and the next 13 days, one page per unit group, one row per date with the crews on and the Days / Nights lines, OPEN in red; a PDF beside the month calendars, then "print as well?".
- **Minimum staffing (kit 2; your numbers of Oct 18).** The **Min Staff** sheet (admin): Unit, Shift, Minimum, Note; blank = no minimum on that shift. Phosphine 2 / 2 (1 per train: the roster can't tell the trains apart), Derivatives 3 / 3, Cypure 1 / 1, Material Handler 1 / 1, QC Derivatives 1 / 1, QC Gases 1 on days only (no afternoon shift in the model), Utilities 1 days only, SBP 1 days only. For today and the next 13 days a short shift gets a red **N short** flag in the calendar's date cell (click the date for which) and a row under **BELOW MINIMUM STAFFING** on Needs Attention (double-click opens the calendar on that date). Staffed = the crew on that shift holding a code in the unit, minus booked off / sick for that shift / off in a switch, plus every coverage line a named person covers (a switch's S/S line counts the one working it). Reads only.
- **Payback aging finished (kit 4).** Needs Attention's SHIFT SWITCH PAYBACKS OWED is oldest first; one owed over 42 days reads "Payback owed N days" in red on amber; the Desk's tile says "(N overdue)".
- **The ID counters checked against the archives (I8).** IntegrityCheck's IDs line reports a PROBLEM when an ID counter is below an archived year's highest number (the next ID could repeat an archived one); `RepairIdMarks` (admin, Alt+F8) raises the counters and never lowers one.
- **Backup housekeeping.** The "Backup before ..." copies beside the roster are now pruned at 30 days with the daily ones (this roster's name only, exact pattern); SystemCheck says how many of each are kept and their size.
- **Attendance:** two columns, Last sick day and That day counted as.
- **The members' file (M3; `INSTALL-members.md`, one template rebuild):** **Am I next?** on the Where I stand card (the next shift they are listed for and their place); **Larger type** (130 %) on Home; My history shows Call order and **Why** for every charge and has **Print / PDF**; a member who moved unit no longer shows a rank or a Sign me up on the old unit's posting; `~` on Who's next for estimated hours; every withdrawal is named at LOAD with its login and time; Sign me up checks the picked name and names its file with the PC; the month picker no longer depends on the PC's date settings; a Reason like "3-4" stays text. The `PUBLISH_REASONS` setting is gone (the Why line replaces it, screened for leave words); a dashed local number like 555-0142 now stops a publish (write times as 0700-1900).
- Settings: none new. The Min Staff sheet is the one place to type the minimums.

## The panel
1. Open the roster (ADMIN view), then File > Open `Roster Updater.xlsm` in the same Excel window. Click the roster so it is in front.
2. Alt+F8 > `'Roster Updater.xlsm'!InstallPanel`. Pick the unzipped `Callout_Roster_v26` folder.
3. Click the one blue button, read its box, click the next. Group 3: the bar rebuild, System check ("25 of 25 on v26"), Self test (644 / 74), the members' template rebuilt, the Min Staff sheet. Group 4: the practice copy + SmokeTest (19 steps), the paybacks colour and the staffing section on Needs Attention, the calendar's "N short" flag, the Board, Employee history (member-safe and full), the Refusals Report's second part and the OT Log's status-bar line, the Attendance columns, IntegrityCheck's IDs line, SystemCheck's backups line, the members' file's new lines, a supervisor's login.

By hand instead: remove and import the 25 modules, Debug > Compile, Alt+F8 > ApplyRosterLayout; open the members' TEMPLATE, Remove + Import `member/modMember.bas`, re-paste `member/ThisWorkbook.txt`, Compile, Alt+F8 > BuildMemberTemplate, Save; then the group 3-4 rows yourself.
