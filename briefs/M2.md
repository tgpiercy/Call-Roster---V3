## 1. Find me
Today `MemberFindMe` redraws the roster A - Z with the name highlighted; the unit pickers on Who's next and Calendar stay where they were.
- Picking a name (Home `C14` or Roster `C2`) looks up **Home unit** in `dRoster` (`RC_HOME`) and:
  - draws the Roster **sorted for that unit** (call order) with the person highlighted and scrolled into view;
  - sets the unit picker on **Who's next** and **Calendar** to it, and the name on **My history**;
  - shows a one-line card on Home: name, crew, home unit, Total / Worked / Refused (call-out, posting), call position in the home unit.
- No home unit published for that name: fall back to today's A - Z view and say "no home unit on file".
- Remember the name **per Windows login** on that PC (`SaveSetting` / `GetSetting`, nothing written to the shared file), so the next open goes straight to their view. A "Not me" link clears it.

## 2. Calendar
**DECIDED: the master's month grid.** Today the members' calendar is one row per date with two text cells.
- Mirror `modCalendar.DrawCalendar` in the master: week rows Sunday to Saturday, each day split into a days half (yellow) and a nights half (blue), date and holiday name in the day's header, one line per need with the same colours for vacation / training / leave ("Leave" only) / OPEN / POSTED / S/S / PHA, weekend shading, today outlined.
- Month and unit pickers stay (`B2`, `D2`); add previous / next month buttons and Print (landscape, one page wide).
- Data stays `dCal` exactly as published (date, shift, unit, off / need, covered by, type, open flag). If the grid needs something `dCal` lacks (for example the rotation crew on days / nights for the day header), add it to the publish in v24.1 rather than working it out in the members' file.
- Read the master's drawing code for sizes and colours; copy the constants, not the logic that decides anything.
- Rows auto-size to the busiest day of the week; a day with more lines than fit shows "+3 more" and the full list in the cell's note or on double-click.

## 3. Sign up
**DECIDED: a sign-up inbox** (format and master behaviour in `briefs/v24.1.md` section 3).
- **Postings** sheet: each open posting the person is listed on gets **Sign me up** and, once signed, **Withdraw**. The pop-up (code-built) shows the posting, the person's name (from Find me; they can pick another name, since plant PCs are shared), whole shift or partial hours, and the closing date and time.
- Clicking writes ONE new file into `SIGNUP_FOLDER` (path published in `dMeta`): never edits or deletes a file, never writes to the members' workbook itself. If the folder can't be reached: say so and tell them to use the paper sheet.
- Refuse in the workbook only what is plainly impossible from published values: posting not open, past the published closing time, hours not a number or not below the posting's hours. Everything else is the master's call.
- After writing, show a receipt line (posting, name, hours, time) and mark the posting "Sign-up sent" by reading the inbox folder for that person's newest file. After the next publish the status comes from the master.
- Out-of-department sign-up: a person not on the posting's list can still send one; the pop-up says it counts only if nobody in the department takes it.
- Setting published by the master: sign-ups on / off. Off = the buttons don't appear and the sheet says "Sign up on the posted sheet".

## 4. Refused split
- `dRoster` gains two columns after Refused (v24.1). Move `RC_PROV`, `RC_FIXED` and anything that counts past them; `DrawRoster` shows `Refused` with `(call-out x / posting y)` under or beside it; My history gets sub-totals by type from `dLog`.
- The template must be rebuilt or updated (`BuildMemberTemplate` again, or an update macro): the INSTALL note says which, and the master's publish must refuse an old template whose column count doesn't match (version check in `dMeta`).

## 5. Where I stand
Once Find me knows who is looking, Home shows a card built only from published values:
- Total / Worked / Refused (call-out, posting), and "data through" from `dMeta`.
- **Call position** in the home unit and in every other unit they hold a code in (from `dRoster`'s per-unit position columns).
- **Open postings I'm listed on:** posting, date, shift, hours, my position on that list, closes when, and my sign-up status with the Sign me up / Withdraw button beside it.
- **Next 14 days:** the dates and shifts where they are in the first three of Who's next for their home unit (from `dNext`).
- Anything not published says "ask your supervisor": the card never works anything out.

- The four checkers on `member/` too. No reserved-word names, no undeclared names: the two compile errors that reached Graeme were in code he had never compiled.

## Acceptance
- Pick a name on Home: Roster shows that person's unit in call order with them highlighted; Who's next and Calendar open on the same unit; closing and reopening on the same login needs no pick.
- Calendar for a month with vacation, a leave, an open need and a posted shift looks like the master's Calendar tab for the same month and unit, side by side.
- Sign me up for a whole shift, then Withdraw, then sign up for 8 h: three files in the inbox; the master's Award pop-up shows the person ticked with 8 h.
- With the inbox folder renamed, Sign me up says to use the paper sheet and writes nothing anywhere.
