# Guide 08 - the second admin's week on the Admin Lite Desk (v29)

Who this is for: Ray (lanthierr), the second admin, who covers the admin chair when Graeme is on nights or away. Everything here is done from the **Admin Lite Desk**, the screen he opens on. The full admin Desk is one click away (**Show full admin Desk >**) and is the same Desk Graeme uses.

## How the Lite Desk is organised
Four bands in fixed places (the one-page sheet `docs/admin-lite-cheat-sheet.md` has them in short). A card that has nothing to do stays where it is, grey, so the eye learns where things live. The trust strip under the title says, grey or red, whether the hours, today's backup, the members' file and the system checks are fine; a red item says **call Graeme**.

## 1. Members' requests (band 1, first card)
A member asked for time off, or a switch with a named partner who has agreed, from the members' file. The card counts the ones waiting for an admin.
1. Click the card: Needs Attention opens with **MEMBERS' REQUESTS** first.
2. **Double-click** the request: the time-off or switch pop-up opens **prefilled**. Every rule applies as if you typed it (bookings, the 16 h stop, the sick window). **Save** books it; the request becomes **Booked** and the member sees that after the next publish.
3. **Right-click** to decline, with a reason the member will read (no leave words: the publish refuses them).
4. If the booking is later voided (Fix an entry, Void time off), the request reads **Booking voided** for the member.

## 2. Posting exceptions (band 1, second card)
An open posting nobody signed up for that has closed, or closes within 24 h. The click opens the **Award pop-up** on the one that has waited longest:
- **No takers** - every eligible person on the list is charged the posting's hours (the rule).
- **Cancel** - nobody is charged.
- **Award Out of Dept** - the out-of-department candidates in call order; the department's eligible people who did not sign up are charged.
Not sure which: the posting's Reason and the Calendar line tell the story; Graeme decides the odd ones.

## 3. Corrections asked for (band 1, third card) - Fix an entry
A receipt was answered **No** or **Not sure**, or a dispute is open. The click opens **Fix an entry**:
1. Pick the entry from the list (the Ref, the answer, the member's line), or type a Ref ID.
2. **Show it**: every row it wrote and whose Total Hours it moved.
3. Type the **reason** (it goes into the Void Note with your login and the time), click **Void this entry**. The void is the roster's own: the rows are marked VOID, the hours come back, the calendar line is released.
4. **Re-enter it now?** opens the right pop-up prefilled (a call-out, an adjustment, a switch, time off). Save it; the receipt's answer is marked resolved with the new Ref. **Close** instead marks it resolved as voided.
An award is not voided here: use **Withdraw an award / edit a posting** on the full Desk.

## 4. Returning from leave (band 1, fourth card)
Someone comes back from STD / LOA / parental leave in the next 14 days, or is on open-ended leave. **Leave > Returning** books the return; the roster works out the top-up (the home unit's average Total Hours gained while they were away) and shows it before saving.

## 5. Book and change (band 2)
Time off, leave, a need on the calendar (right-click the day), a person (new hire, unit move, rename, remove). The pop-ups are the same as the full Desk's.

## 6. The daily check (band 3) - only when Graeme is off
1. **Import Kronos** - the morning report in the Kronos folder (the pill in the header turns red when the data is old).
2. **Clear Needs Attention** - look at every line; book, decline, award, apply or leave it.
3. **Publish the members' file** - so the members see today's state.
Each step turns green once anyone has done it today; when all three are green the band is one line naming who did them and when.

## 7. Staffing and OT (band 4)
The shifts under the Min Staff minimum in the next 14 days (the click opens the Calendar on the first), and this pay period's OT hours charged by unit as bars. **Staffing outlook** is the Calendar; **Look up a person** the employee history (member-safe or full).

## 8. The first week
The strip under the Desk ticks five things as you do them: book time off, approve a member's request, resolve a posting exception, fix an entry, run the daily check. It shows for your first three sessions on the Lite Desk; **Hide this** puts it away.

## 9. When something goes wrong
- A red trust-strip item: call Graeme; the item says what.
- A box says "Something went wrong": the entry it names was or was not saved (the box says); click **Email Graeme the details**.
- The file says it is read-only: someone else has it open; close and reopen.
- The file says it **moved**: open the new path it names (go-live week only).

## 10. Practice first
**Practice on a copy** (the grey row) makes a COPY file: everything above can be tried in it; nothing done there reaches the roster or the members' file. The rehearsal week (Dec 7-11) runs on such a copy.
