## Decisions (Graeme, Oct 14, 2026)
- **D-H3** cancel of a planned crew change before its date: **delete the never-effective future crew row**; a cancel after the date dates the undo today and says so.
- **D-H4/H5** due moves: a Needs Attention line applies **only the clicked move**; the box is **two plain questions** ("Apply it now?" Yes/No; only on No, "Cancel the move?" Yes/No).
- **D-C7** the one date parser: **rollover rule everywhere** ("Jan 5" in December = next January), times stripped, unambiguous pre-fills; **a return date in the future is refused**.
- **D-E3** out-of-department candidates: **the call order, grade first**; the hours-only re-sort goes.
- **D-C1** a partial-day absence **blocks both shifts of the date** (as today).
- **D-C2/C3** a shift GIVEN UP in a switch: **not free for the other shift that day** (as today), but the given-up shift **relieves the rest rule next day** (they did not work it): `NotFreeReason` / `RotationFreeReason` take a switch-off day out of the rest test. (Confirmed in the go-ahead prompt.)
- **D-L1** the 24 h sick window: **may neither work nor give up a shift inside it** (switch pick list and form refuse).
- **D-E4** (read back and corrected): **a partial acceptance CLOSES the call-out shift.** Once a partial Accepted is logged in a session, the pop-up refuses a further Accepted (above or below); the remaining hours are dropped, uncharged, and nobody below the partial is offered or charged for them. This REVERSES the v24.1 rule ("everyone below is offered what is left and charged on the hours offered"): `CalloutOffers` changes, with cases; CLAUDE.md's charging table row is rewritten. Refusals logged BEFORE the partial keep their charge on the hours offered to them.
Defaults taken unless Graeme says otherwise: D-P1 (a late sign-up action never changes a tick; dormant), D-P3 (not chargeable, named), D-E1/E2 (IntegrityCheck reports; no cell edits by code), D-L2 (a run crossing Jan 1 = a new occurrence, as built), D-L5 (sick over vacation saves silently), D-H2 (apply during a leave; the return top-up measured from the move date), D-M3/M4/M5 (sign-ups: dormant; `PUBLISH_REASONS` False for good).
