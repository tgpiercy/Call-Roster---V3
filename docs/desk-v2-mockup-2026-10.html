Proposed: Desk v2

Four bands instead of six. Three colours plus grey: orange for right now, green for planning, navy for finishing. The header's three facts become three pills. Each tile names the next thing and says what a click does. Help, Undo and the links share one quiet footer row. Nothing the boss has rights to is removed.

What changes and why

ChangeTodayDesk v2Why
BandsSix (RIGHT NOW, PEOPLE, HELP, PLAN AHEAD, WHEN YOU ARE DONE, plus TODAY)Four (TODAY, NOW, PLAN, FINISH), three side by sideThe eye finds the band first, then the button. Three columns keep every button above the fold.
ColoursSeven on buttons (orange, green, navy, purple, amber, blue, grey)One per band: orange, green, navy; grey for helpColour then means "when", not "which pop-up".
HeaderOne 11 pt line with bar separatorsThree pills; the Kronos pill turns red and says "import it" when the data is oldThree facts read in one glance; the warning stands alone.
TilesA count and a caption; the cover tile names the next shiftA count, the next thing (the posting closing soonest, the overdue count), and the action a click takesThe tile is the task, not a statistic.
Help and UndoTwo bands in the left columnOne footer row of grey buttons with the links and the keysThey are reached for rarely; a landing page should lead with the work.
LeaveIts own PEOPLE bandIn PLANIt is planned, like time off.
RightsFull admin access; Show everythingUnchangedThe boss keeps every tool. Only grouping and colour change.

Not in this mock-up, by decision: the pop-ups' type size (UI_SCALE) and the call-out form's fit on the plant screen. Those wait for the screen measurement. Built as a UI-only item (code redraw, no form re-paste) after the boss's first alpha week.
