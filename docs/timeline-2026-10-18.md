## Documentation and the what-if scenarios (Nov 1 - 15, during alpha)
Written while alpha runs so that the beta presentation and the Dec 1 training use them:
1. **The what-if scenario sheets**: one page each, the situation in plain words, what the supervisor does (which button, which pop-up), what the roster does (the rule in one line), what the members see, how to check it on a practice copy. The set: a sick call on nights and the call-out from it; a call-out with a refusal, a left message and a partial acceptance; a posting from new to award, with a sign-up and an outside award; no takers; a shift switch and its payback; time off that needs cover; a leave going and returning; a planned unit move; a new hire and someone leaving; a DST night; the members' file published; a plant holiday; the year-end rollover; "something went wrong".
2. **The guides refreshed** for v25.5 / v26: the supervisor quick guide, the Desk guide, the admin install guide, the members' guide (`docs/INSTALL-members.md` and the members' front page notes).
3. **The presentation pack for Nov 15**: what the roster does, the rules in one page (call order, charging, who is free, privacy), what the members' file shows, the before / after numbers, what alpha found and fixed, the December plan.
4. **The training plan for Dec 1**: 45 minutes per supervisor on a practice copy, the scenario sheets as the script, the Desk first.
