What changes and why

ChangeToday (M3.1)M4Why
Order of HomeButtons, then Find me, then the cardMe first: name, since-you-last-looked, four tiles, then the buttonsPeople open the file to see where they stand.
Remember mePick the name every timeRemembered per PC user in Excel's own setting store; "Not me" clears itNothing written to the shared file; no login stored in it.
HeaderEach sheet its own; Larger type on Home only; back through the tabsOne band on all sheets: title, published stamp, Larger type, HomeThe same place for the same things.
Wording"Refused", "#4 (D2)""Charged (refusals)", "4th of 14 in Phosphine"Plain words; a position means more with the list size.
Highlight meThe name read among the rowsThe member's row shaded on Who's next, the Roster and the CalendarOne glance for "where am I".
Sign me up from HomePostings sheet, then the rowThe next open posting for me on the Am I next? tile with its own buttonOne click from opening the file.
Since you last lookedNothingNew postings in my units, awards, my position change since this PC user's last openThe file answers "what changed" instead of being re-read.
How it worksThe paper guideA Help sheet in the file: the call order, why I was charged, Exempt and the tilde, sign-ups, the rest ruleHelp that is always in the file; the Why lines explain each charge, this explains the rules once.
Publish digest by emailNoneAt publish, each member with an email on file gets their own tiles and their units' open postingsReaches people who do not open the file. Only their own figures; leaves the shared folder, so the wording is checked by you first.
