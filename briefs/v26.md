# v26 - the BETA BUILD: the reports and the members' follow-up (M3)

Written Oct 18, 2026 from the kit brief (`docs/code-kit-2026-10/briefs/07-v26-reporting.md`), the deep review's sections 4.5 and 4.6 (`docs/deep-review-2026-10-13.md`) and `docs/timeline-2026-10-18.md`: **v26 ships by Nov 15** and is presented to management and the union that day. It holds the reports (the employee history, "why was I charged", the two-week board print, payback aging, the IdMark-vs-archive integrity line, backup housekeeping, `LastReason` on Attendance), the members' template rebuilt (M3, one members' install) and **whatever alpha testing (Nov 1-15) turns up** (the v25.5.5 bundle folds in, as the v25.3.5 bundle folded into v25.5). The numbering rule: v26 is the major revision; revisions after the Nov 15 review are v26.5 and count up.

**Rule changes: none.** Every charge, call order and who-is-free rule stays where it is. The new code READS the OT Log, the posting lists, the switch table, the backup folder and the archive sheets, and writes output-only sheets, PDFs, the status bar, one more Needs Attention colour, two Attendance columns and the members' tables. The only new writes to data: the backup prune (deletes dated copies only, by exact name pattern, as the daily prune does), the optional `RepairIdMarks` (raises an ID counter; it never lowers one) and the Min Staff sheet made on first use (a settings table an admin types into).
