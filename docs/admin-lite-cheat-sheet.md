# Admin Lite Desk - one page (v29)

For Ray. You open on this Desk. It shows your jobs in four bands that never move. **Show full admin Desk >** (top right) has everything else; **< Back to my Desk** returns. Nothing here changes what you may do: you are an admin.

## The top two lines
- **ADMIN LITE | Signed in as Ray - admin rights | Show full admin Desk >**
- **The trust strip** - four facts, grey when fine, **red with "call Graeme"** when not: the hours (Kronos through which day, plus how many provisional), today's backup, when the members' file was last updated, the system checks.

## Band 1 - NEEDS YOUR DECISION (oldest first; one decision per card; a grey card says "Nothing waiting")
| Card | What it counts | What the click does |
|---|---|---|
| **Member requests** | Requests waiting for an admin (and how many still wait for a partner's Agree) | Needs Attention: **double-click** a request to book it through the pop-up (prefilled), **right-click** to decline with a reason |
| **Posting exceptions** | Open postings nobody signed up for, past closing or closing within 24 h | The Award pop-up on the oldest: **No takers** (everyone on the list is charged), **Cancel** (nobody), **Award Out of Dept** |
| **Corrections asked for** | Receipts answered No / Not sure, plus open disputes | **Fix an entry**: pick the Ref, see what it wrote, **Void** with a reason, **Re-enter it now?** |
| **Returning from leave** | People back from leave in the next 14 days, or on open-ended leave | **Leave > Returning** - the return and its top-up |

## Band 2 - BOOK AND CHANGE
- **Book time off** - vacation, personal days, a booked sick day.
- **Leave: going or returning** - STD, LOA, parental.
- **Add a need** - the Calendar opens on today; right-click the day > Add a need.
- **Change a person** - new hire, unit move, rename, remove (the Employee pop-up).

## Band 3 - DAILY CHECK (only when Graeme is off)
Three steps, each **green once anyone has done it today**; one green line when all three are done.
1. **Import Kronos** - the morning report.
2. **Clear Needs Attention** - look at every line (it shows how many shifts need cover).
3. **Publish the members' file.**

## Band 4 - STAFFING AND OT
- **Below minimum in the next 14 days** - the count and the first shifts; the click opens the Calendar on the first one.
- **OT hours charged this pay period, by unit** - bars, display only.
- **Staffing outlook (calendar)**, **Look up a person (history)**.

## The grey row at the foot
How do I ... (Help), Something went wrong, Suggest an improvement, Undo my last log entry, Practice on a copy, Larger type.

## When something is red
The trust strip's red item says what: **call Graeme**. If the roster will not open or save: close WITHOUT saving, reopen, and call.

## The "?" on each band
Opens the band's plain-language lines (the same words as this sheet).
