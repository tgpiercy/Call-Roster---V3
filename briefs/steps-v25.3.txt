# Format: kind | caption | argument | flag or expected result.  Dates in a test's argument: +N = today plus N days.

[3. Run once]
macro   | Rebuild the roster's bar (the "< Desk" button)                  | ApplyRosterLayout
macro   | System check (expect 25 of 25 on v25.3, ThisWorkbook v25.3)      | SystemCheckOK
macro   | Self test (expect 575 passed, 68 suites)                           | SelfTestOK
manual  | DESK_USERS                                                        | In the VBA editor (Alt+F11), modOvertimeEntry near the top: DESK_USERS = "lanthierr" is the placeholder. Set it to your boss's Windows login (the one ADMIN_USERS carries for him), Debug > Compile, Ctrl+S. If the login is right already, click Done.

[4. Checks]
macro   | Make a practice copy (it opens; leave it for the next row)        | MakePracticeCopy | confirm
manual  | Smoke test in the COPY (21 steps)                                 | In the practice copy: Alt+F8 > SmokeTest > OK. Expect SMOKE TEST PASSED, 21 steps. Step 21 is new (the Desk's figures: cover / ready / owed before, during and after a vacation and a posting). Then CLOSE THE COPY WITHOUT SAVING and come back to this sheet. Click Done.
manual  | The Desk from your own login                                      | OT Roster, ADMIN view: the PRINT / PUBLISH group has a "< Desk" button under Calendar. Click it: the Desk tab appears first, with the header (today, the pay period, the Kronos line), four tiles and fourteen buttons; the admin-only tabs go (Absences, Coverage, Holidays, the logs): you see the Desk, the tabs a supervisor sees, and Needs Attention. Click "Someone called in sick": the Sick Call pop-up opens; Cancel it. Click each tile: it goes where its caption says. Click "Something went wrong": an email draft opens with the newest Error Log line (or a box with the text). Click "Show everything": the full bar and tabs come back. "< Desk" again. Then Full roster > at the bottom.
manual  | The supervisor Desk: the layout                                   | Alt+F11, Ctrl+G (the Immediate window), type  ApplyView False  and Enter, then click the Desk tab: the supervisor's layout (Someone called in sick, Fill a shift now, What needs cover, Post a shift, Award a posting, Print the call-out roster, the two help buttons; no paybacks tile, no time off / switch / leave / publish, no "Show everything"). Full roster >: the bar shows Sick Call, New Posting, Award Posting and Call Out Roster. Then Alt+F8 > LoginFullAccess to come back. (Your login still passes every admin gate: the gates are the next row.)
manual  | The supervisor Desk: the gates                                    | On a practice copy opened under a SUPERVISOR's login (not yours): it opens on the Desk; "Someone called in sick" opens the Sick Call pop-up (Cancel); "Post a shift" opens New Posting (Cancel); "Award a posting" opens the award pop-up and, with a posting loaded, its No Takers / Award Out of Dept / Cancel Request buttons say "Access denied" while Submit Award is allowed; "Print the call-out roster" draws the sheet. If no supervisor can open a copy today, click Done and do this row when one can: its result goes in the note.
manual  | Save and reopen in the Desk view                                  | With the Desk showing, Ctrl+S, close the roster, open it again from your login: it opens as before (your login is not on DESK_USERS). Open the file as your boss, or watch him open it: it lands on the Desk; the roster and the Calendar show at 120 %.
