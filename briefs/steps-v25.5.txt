manual  | The call-out pop-up: the keys                                     | In the pop-up the last row opened (or open it again: Fill a shift now on the Desk), type 12 hours and LOAD LIST on a practice copy. The card shows the first person (name 16 pt, phone 24 pt, one line under). Press A: the row reads Accepted and the next person is on the card. R, L, U (the focus goes to the reason list: pick one, Enter). K skips one (its row keeps "-"), B goes back one, P asks for part of the hours (Cancel it). Click Call back: the left-message person comes up. The progress line reads "N of M logged - 1 left message ... - started N min ago". Press Esc: "unsaved entries will be lost" - No. Then SUBMIT ALL ENTRIES on the copy only: the box is four lines; the status bar at the bottom holds the detail. Close the pop-up. Click Done; note anything under 10 pt or cut off.
