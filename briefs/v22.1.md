# v22.1 - Sick and personal day tracking; vacation on scheduled shifts

Requested by Graeme on Oct 12, 2026, before the v23 rollover. Install needs v22.

## Why
The plant's attendance rule is tracked by hand today. The roster already records every sick shift (v22 `SC-####` rows) and every booking, so it can keep the counters and say, at the moment a sick call is logged, whether that shift is paid. Separately, Book Time Off lets a vacation range cover days the person isn't scheduled, which then blocks them from OT on their days off.

## The rule (as Graeme stated it, Oct 12)
- Each employee has **5 paid sick days** a year, usable in **up to 3 occurrences**, never more than 5 days in total. A sick day past the 5th day or past the 3rd occurrence is unpaid.
- Each employee also has **5 unpaid personal days** a year.
- A **day** is one scheduled 12-hour shift (a partial sick shift still counts as a day). An **occurrence** is a run of consecutive scheduled shifts missed sick; days off between them don't break the run.
- The year is the **calendar year** (Jan 1).
- When sick + personal days used pass **5**, the employee is **not entitled to paid sick days** from that day **until one year after the first day of their 2nd occurrence** (their only occurrence if there was just one). On that anniversary the counters reset to 5 / 5 for the rest of the calendar year, and the Jan 1 cycle resumes.
- Vacation is booked **only on scheduled shifts**.

Nothing in this update changes Total Hours, call order or charging. It is counters, flags and messages.

## Decisions (Graeme, Oct 12)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Day / occurrence | Shift = day; consecutive scheduled shifts = one occurrence; partial shift = a full day |
| Year basis | Calendar year, Jan 1 |
| Wait rule | 1 year after the 2nd occurrence (the only one if just one), then a full reset |
| Vacation | Check against the person's shift schedule |
| Personal day label (members) | "Personal" |
| Back-fill past sick days | Yes: Book Time Off gets `SIC - Sick day (past)` |
| Sick call on an unscheduled shift | Counts as a day |
| Reset after the 1-year wait | Fresh counters to Dec 31, then the Jan 1 cycle |

## Build
### 1. One rule function: `modAttendance.SickStatus` (new module, mirrored in `tests/rules.py`)
Input: the employee's non-void `SIC` rows (date + shift) and `PER` bookings (ranges), the schedule (`EmpShiftOn`), and an as-of date. Output (a `Public Type AttendanceStatus`):
`YearStart`, `PaidUsed`, `PaidLeft`, `Occurrences` (count), `OccurrenceDates` (first day of each), `PersonalUsed`, `PersonalLeft`, `Combined`, `Entitled` (Boolean), `WaitUntil` (Date, 0 = none), `Reason` (plain words).
- Sick days = count of `SIC` rows with shift date in the entitlement year (a row per shift; the v22 Sick Call form already writes one per shift).
- Occurrences: sort the sick shifts; a sick shift joins the previous occurrence when no scheduled shift of that person falls between them that wasn't sick.
- Paid: the first 5 sick days inside the first 3 occurrences. The 6th day, or any day in a 4th occurrence, is unpaid.
- Personal days = scheduled shifts inside `PER` bookings in the year.
- Entitlement: from the day Combined > 5, `Entitled = False` and `WaitUntil` = first day of the 2nd occurrence + 1 year (or of the only one). From `WaitUntil`, counters restart (the entitlement year begins at `WaitUntil` and runs to Dec 31; from the next Jan 1 the normal year resumes). The prior year's state carries forward: someone who crossed 5 in November is still waiting on Jan 1.
- Last year's rows are read only to find `WaitUntil`; nothing older than the previous calendar year matters.

### 2. Personal days
- New absence type `PER - Personal day (unpaid)` in `AbsenceTypes` (Book Time Off). Calendar tag "Personal", colour grey-blue; counts in `AbsentOn`/`NotFreeReason` like any booking (off call lists on those days).
- Members' calendar shows "Personal" too (it isn't a leave code; `PublicType` passes it through).

### 3. Sick Call form
- When the person is picked, a status line: "2026: 3 of 5 paid sick used in 2 of 3 occurrences; personal 1 of 5. This shift: PAID" or "This shift: UNPAID - 6th day" / "UNPAID - not entitled until Mar 14, 2027 (over 5 days on Nov 2, 2026)".
- Saving writes the outcome into the absence Note ("Paid sick day 4 of 5, occurrence 2" / "Unpaid - ..."), so the row says what the system decided that day. The counters are still derived from the rows, never from the notes.

### 4. Book Time Off
- `SIC - Sick day (past)` added to the type list for back-filling this year's sick days taken before v22: a range becomes one `SC-####` row per scheduled shift in it (same rows the Sick Call form writes, no OPEN line, no call-out).
- Vacation (`VAC`) and personal (`PER`): the save is refused when the range covers no scheduled shift ("covers no scheduled shift for Jane Doe - check the dates or crew"). When the range starts or ends on a day off, the form offers to trim it to the first/last scheduled shift ("Oct 13-19 covers 4 shifts; Oct 13 and Oct 19 are days off. Trim to Oct 14-18?"). Yes trims, No saves as typed, Cancel goes back. Other types (training, leave) are unchanged: leave is a continuous absence.

### 5. Attendance view (admin only)
- PEOPLE group gets an **Attendance** button: draws an admin sheet `Attendance` (one row per employee: Home Unit, Crew, paid sick used / left, occurrences, personal used / left, combined, status, wait-until, last sick date), sorted by status then name. Hidden from members, never published.
- Needs Attention: new section **ATTENDANCE** listing anyone over 5 combined, anyone in the wait, and anyone at 5 days or 3 occurrences (one line each; double-click opens the Attendance sheet).

### 6. Plumbing
- `N_MODULES = 15` (`modAttendance`); SystemCheck checks it and that `tblAbsence` has the v22 columns (SIC rows need the Shift column).
- SelfTest suite `attend` from `tests/cases.csv`: single day, partial day, run across days off, 3rd occurrence boundary, 6th day unpaid, over-5 wait date, reset at the anniversary, personal days from a range, prior-year carry-over. Python mirror `rules.sick_status`.
- Version stamps: modAttendance, modCalendar, modUI, modSystem, modSelfTest, frmTimeOff, frmSickCall -> `v22.1`. `EXPECTED_RELEASE = "v22.1"`. Zip `dist/Callout_Roster_v22.1.zip` (full set), tag `v22.1`.

## Privacy
Sick and personal counts, the Attendance sheet and the Needs Attention lines are admin-only. Members see "Sick" / "Personal" on calendar lines as now, never counts or reasons. Nothing in the OT Log changes.

## Out of scope
Pay itself (Kronos/payroll decide pay; the roster records what the rule says), doctor's notes, WSIB/STD interaction with the counters (a leave is not a sick day), the v23 rollover.
