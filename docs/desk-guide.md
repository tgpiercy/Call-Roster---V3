# The Desk - one page (v25.5)

The Desk is the first tab you see when you open the roster. It has tiles that say what is waiting today, and buttons grouped by when you need them. Every button opens the same pop-up the full roster uses; nothing is different underneath.

There are two Desks from the same screen. **A supervisor's Desk** (a login on the supervisor list, v25.5) has the jobs a supervisor may do: Someone called in sick, Fill a shift now, What needs cover, Post a shift, Award a posting, Update the members' file, Print the call-out roster, and the help buttons. **The admin Desk** (for a login on the Desk list) has those plus Book time off, Shift switch or payback, Leave, Undo my last log entry, the paybacks-owed tile and "Show everything". The header says which crews are on today; the cover tile names the next uncovered shift.

A grey tile means nothing to do. A coloured one has work in it.

## The buttons
- **RIGHT NOW**: Someone called in sick (the Sick Call pop-up: book the sick shift and, if you want, start the call-out from it). Fill a shift now (the Log Call-Out pop-up: pick the unit, date and shift, load the list, call down it). What needs cover (the Calendar).
- **PLAN AHEAD**: Post a shift (New Posting). Award a posting. Book time off. Shift switch or payback.
- **PEOPLE**: Leave: going or returning. Undo my last log entry (v25.5: voids your newest OT Log entry of today through Void Entry's confirm and reason).
- **WHEN YOU ARE DONE**: Update the members' file (Member View). Print the call-out roster.
- **HELP**: How do I ... (the Help tab inside the file: one heading and a paragraph per job; < Back returns here). Something went wrong (an email to Graeme with the newest Error Log line already in it - add what you were doing and send). Suggest an improvement (the same kind of email). Under them the keys: Ctrl+Shift+S sick call, Ctrl+Shift+C call-out, Ctrl+Shift+K calendar, Ctrl+Shift+D this Desk.

## The tabs
You see the **Desk** and the tabs every supervisor sees (OT Roster, Calendar, Postings, OT Log, Shift Switches ...); the admin Desk adds **Needs Attention**, and opens the roster and the Calendar at 120 % so they read easily. The admin-only tabs (Absences, Coverage, Holidays, the logs) are in the file but out of the way.

## If you need the full roster tools (admin Desk)
Click **Show everything (primary admin tools)** at the bottom of the Desk. The full bar and every tab appear for this session. **< Desk** on the bar brings the Desk back. Nothing is saved differently either way. On a supervisor's Desk, **Full roster >** goes to the roster and the Desk tab brings you back.

## If a red "This didn't finish" box appears
Click OK, then **Something went wrong** on the Desk: the email it opens has the details. Whatever was done before the error is still in the file.
