manual  | The 16 h rule: max N h on the list (in the COPY)                  | In a practice copy: Fill a shift now for tonight's Nights, accept the FULL 12 h for someone (A), SUBMIT. Fill a shift now again for TOMORROW's Days, LOAD LIST: that person shows [max 4 h] on the row and "16 h RULE: can take at most 4 h" on the card; A is refused with a box, P with 4 is accepted. Award a posting: a person in the same position shows "max 4 h (16 h rule)" and a full award is refused. Close the copy without saving. Click Done.

Tell <admin> to import Kronos." An admin still gets the Yes / No (Yes the default). If no stale copy can be had today, click Done and note it.

Click Done.
