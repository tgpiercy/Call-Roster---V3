[4. Checks]
manual  | Draw times: the Calendar                                          | ADMIN view, OT Roster tab: click the roster's Calendar BUTTON (not the sheet tab: the button is what times the switch sync). On the Calendar pick the BUSIEST month in the month list (the one with the most lines), then click Next and Prev so it is drawn fresh. The hint under the title ends "Drawn in N s." Then Alt+F8 > ShowCalDrawTimes: the box gives the Calendar's breakdown (caches, wipe, header, weeks = slots / notes / cells, page, controls, then relock + recalc) and the Open path line. Type the Calendar line AND the Open path line into this row's note, or photograph the box. Also time one Next-month click with a stopwatch, from the click to the screen settling, and put that figure in the note too ("drawn in" is the draw alone; the stopwatch is what you feel).
manual  | Draw times: Needs Attention                                       | ADMIN view, Needs Attention tab, change its range list to the longest range: it redraws. Alt+F8 > ShowCalDrawTimes again: the second line is the Needs Attention breakdown. Type it into the note or photograph the box. These two lines are the "before" numbers for v25.2.
