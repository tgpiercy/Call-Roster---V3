Employee > Move to unit with a date after today changes nothing on the roster and writes one Planned row. Lists built for a shift on or after that date already treat the person as moved: `LoadEligible` asks `UnitCodeOn` for each code, so they are off the cleared units' lists and on the new unit's with the planned grade code. Call-outs, posting lists, out-of-department candidates, the printed roster and the switch pick list follow because they already pass the shift date.

A posting made in that window freezes the estimate on its list, as it freezes everyone's hours, and its OT Log rows record that posted figure.
- **Applied on open.** The first admin open on or after the date runs every due move through `ComputeUnitMove` (hours as of the effective date) and `SaveUnitMove`: one backup, one save, one box. A move that can't be applied stays Planned with the reason in its Note. `ApplyPlannedMoves` (Alt+F8) does it by hand.
- **Cancel, never edit.** Needs Attention has a "Planned unit moves" block; double-click a line to cancel it (or apply it when due). A cancel removes only that move's `Exempt - moves <date> (PM-000n)` marks from open postings and puts back a crew change entered with it. Postings made while the move was waiting keep their frozen lists: on the new unit's the person is marked `Exempt - move cancelled (PM-000n)` so they are never charged (the mark comes off if the move is entered again or they move there later), and the box names the old unit's postings made without them and the new unit's postings that list them at the planned code and hours.
- **Crew dated at entry.** A crew change that comes with a planned move is written to the Crew History when the move is entered, dated the day of the move, so "scheduled" and the rest rule are right for later shifts.
- **Rename** carries the planned row; **Remove** cancels it first; a **second move** for the same person is refused until the first is cancelled or applied.
- **Postings.** The out-of-department award's "signed off in the unit" test and Reopen Posting's "no longer holds a code" test read the code as of the shift date; Reopen drops the mark of a move that was cancelled since.
- **Members' file.** Who's next loads its lists per date when a planned move falls inside its window; otherwise the publish is unchanged. Nothing else about a planned move is published.
- **IntegrityCheck** check 11: a waiting move for someone not on the roster, to an unknown unit, with no date, or more than 3 days overdue. **SystemCheck** shows how many are waiting.
- **SmokeTest** has 14 steps: step 9 plans a move for tomorrow, checks the roster row is untouched and both units' lists follow the date, cancels it and checks the lists go back.
- **Roster Updater v3** (`updater/modUpdater.bas`; re-import it into Roster Updater.xlsm by hand). v2's first real run (`UpdateMemberTemplate`, Oct 13) said "No roster is open" with the roster open. ## v21 - call order accuracy (Oct 2026)
