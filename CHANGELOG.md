# Changelog

## v22.1 - sick and personal day tracking (Oct 2026)
Brief: `briefs/v22.1.md`. Install: `INSTALL.md` in `dist/Callout_Roster_v22.1.zip` (full set).

- **The attendance rule in one place** (`modAttendance.AttendanceCore`, mirrored in `tests/rules.py`, 18 SelfTest cases): 5 paid sick days a calendar year in up to 3 occurrences (a day = one scheduled shift, partial still counts; an occurrence = consecutive scheduled shifts missed sick), 5 unpaid personal days; past 5 sick + personal days the person is not entitled to paid sick until one year after the first day of their 2nd occurrence (the only one, or the crossing day, if fewer), then the counters restart to Dec 31 and the Jan 1 cycle resumes. A wait carries over Jan 1, and every past row is replayed (a wait set one year restarts the next year part-way). Nothing changes Total Hours, call order, charging or the OT Log.
- **Sick Call** shows the person's counters and whether the shift being logged is paid, and writes that outcome into the absence Note (the counters are always re-derived from the rows).
- **Book Time Off:** new type `PER - Personal day (unpaid)` (calendar "Personal"; off call lists like any booking; members see "Personal"); new type `SIC - Sick day (past)` to back-fill this year's sick days (one row per scheduled shift, oldest first); vacation and personal days are booked on scheduled shifts only: a range with none is refused, days off at either end offer a trim (Yes trims, No saves as typed).
- **Attendance** sheet (ADMIN button, next to Full Access): everyone's counters and status, worst first; admin only, hidden in saved copies, never published. **Needs Attention** gains an ATTENDANCE section for anyone at or over a limit (double-click opens the sheet).
- SystemCheck: 15 modules on v22.1; forms and ThisWorkbook are now checked against the release that last changed each one (no false "re-paste" warnings). Every module carries a v22.1 stamp; only frmTimeOff and frmSickCall changed among the forms.
- Known limit: occurrence grouping follows the crew rotation, not shift switches; the Sick Call counters read the Absences table, so sick days before v22 count only once back-filled.

## v22 - sick calls and leave (Oct 2026)
Brief: `briefs/v22.md`. Install: `INSTALL.md` in `dist/Callout_Roster_v22.zip` (full set).

- **Total Hours as of a date** (`TotalHoursAsOf`, modLeave): the Hours History snapshot with the latest data-through on or before the date, plus non-void charges with shift date on or before it (this year), plus provisional accepted OT as of that snapshot. Estimated (and said so) when no snapshot is early enough.
- **Leave** button: Going books the absence (shown as "Leave" everywhere outside Absences; WSIB and LTD added), locks in the Home Unit and a leave group `LV-####`. Returning works out the top-up = the home unit's average Total Hours gained between the first day off and the return (grade 2 and below, not the returner, not on leave themselves, with Hours History before the window; window clipped to the year start), shows peers, dates, snapshots and before -> after, then logs ONE Adjustment (Ref `LV-####`, Reason "Leave return", Note = the calculation) and closes the booking. Admin override with a required reason. Void Entry `LV-####` undoes the hours. Needs Attention's LEAVE lines offer "Return from leave now".
- **Sick Call** button: books the sick shift (Absence `SC-####`, type SIC, shift recorded), keeps the person off every shift starting within 24 h after it ends, puts an OPEN line on the calendar ("Sick"), and opens Log Call-Out pre-filled.
- **Convert imports** button: old-calendar vacation lines become real bookings (name matched by first name + last initial, consecutive dates merged, each line stamped with its booking so it is never converted twice); Needs Attention lists IMPORTED, NOT BOOKED lines for the next 56 days; SystemCheck flags them.
- `tblAbsence` gains Home Unit, Leave Group and Shift (appended; `UpgradeAbsenceColumns`, verified). PEOPLE group on the command bar. SystemCheck: 14 modules, all forms.
- SelfTest 182 cases (+35): sick window both ways, hours-as-of (snapshot choice, year clip, last year's snapshots ignored, void, night coverage), peer rules, average (rounds half away from zero), run merging. Python mirror 183.
- Review fixes before release: a leave can't be topped up twice (a non-void `LV-####` row blocks it; the Returning list and the Needs Attention menu drop it); "OLV - Other leave" is a real leave code (masked, purple, excluded as a peer); snapshots dated before the year start are last year's and count as 0 worked at the reset; sick calls sit on their booked shift in the calendar (no second OPEN line); when Hours History starts after the first day off (it began Oct 9, 2026) every peer's start figure is estimated from the first snapshot and the confirm says so, and a 0-peer result can't be saved without an override; Convert imports skips a run that overlaps an existing booking and says so; the Absences upgrade compares the whole roster totals block (G:AS).
- `BACKUP_FOLDER` now defaults to `"."` = the folder the roster is in (`BackupFolderPath` also resolves `".\sub"`), so daily backups are on from this release. Point it at a network folder later if wanted.
- Known limit: the 24 h sick window applies to call lists and postings (`NotFreeReason`). The shift-switch pick list and the Booked-off flag still use the sick date only.

## v21 - call order accuracy (Oct 2026)
Brief: `briefs/v21.md`. Install notes: `INSTALL.md` in `dist/Callout_Roster_v21.zip`.

- **Provisional accepted OT.** An Accepted call-out or Awarded posting counts in Total Hours (G) from the moment it is logged, until Kronos covers the shift. The amount is the row's shift hours (not x 1.75), non-void rows in the roster year only. A day shift is covered once its date is on or before the Kronos data-through date; a night shift once the next day is. The import stores the data-through date (file date minus one) on every Hours History row and in the hidden name `KronosDataThrough`; column C on the roster (hidden) holds the provisional total per person and G = H + I + C. The call-out form marks such totals with `*`, the posting form says how many, the printed posting footer says so.
- **DST nights.** The night starting the Saturday before the first Sunday in November is 13 h; the Saturday before the second Sunday in March is 11 h (worked out by rule for any year). New Posting and Log Call-Out default Hours to the actual length on those nights when the shift is Nights and show a warning. Setting `DST_USE_ACTUAL_HOURS` (True).
- **Home Unit** column (B) on the roster with a drop-down of the eight units. `SetupHomeUnit` fills in anyone with a code in exactly one unit; SystemCheck and Needs Attention list the rest. Used by v22 and v24; nothing else reads it yet.
- **Adjustment types.** Add Adjustment asks for a type (Leave return / Unit move / New hire / Correction / Other), stored in the OT Log's Reason column.
- **Grade change log.** Changing a unit code on the roster writes a row to `tblGradeChange` (hidden admin sheet Grade Log; `ShowGradeLog`): timestamp, employee, unit, old code, new code, Windows login. Sorting, filtering and macro writes create no entries.
- **Daily backup.** The first admin open each day saves `Call Out Roster yyyy-mm-dd.xlsm` into `BACKUP_FOLDER` and deletes copies older than 30 days there. Never blocks opening; SystemCheck says when the folder isn't set or can't be reached.
- **SelfTest** (Alt+F8): 147 known-answer cases over rotation, holidays, DST, call order, charging, eligibility and provisional coverage, in memory only. The same table (`tests/cases.csv`) drives the Python mirror in `tests/`.
- **Printed call out roster.** The Call Out Roster button redraws the Blank Call Out Roster sheet from the data: title block with pay period and Kronos status, one section per unit in call order (#, Hours with `*` for provisional, Name [code], Crew, Phone), page setup done, then offers to print. It replaces the old macro that sorted the roster unit by unit and copied the chunks. Roster column A is hidden (empty); B shows Home Unit.
- **Log Call-Out form:** the rotation line is two lines high and wraps (it clipped once the DST note or the crew note was added); the Crew box fits "All not scheduled". Every form now carries a `@version` line and SystemCheck checks all five.
- **SystemCheck** expects v21 on 12 modules and also flags Total Hours without the provisional term, blank Home Units and the backup.
- **Roster writes in this release** (all disclosed): `RefreshRosterFormulas` rewrites C and G formulas (verified, save blocked on a mismatch); `SetupHomeUnit` writes column B and its B2 title (backup first, verified); `ApplyRosterLayout` writes the hidden C2 title "PROV" and, once Total includes the provisional term, rewrites C and G with the period formulas. No hours are ever typed into roster cells.
- Rules consolidated: the call-out charge is one function (`CalloutCharge`); the rotation part of eligibility is `RotationFreeReason`; out-of-department and cancel charging are `ComputeOutside` / `ComputeCancel`.
